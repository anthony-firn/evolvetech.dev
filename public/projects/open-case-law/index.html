Open Case Law

Search the text. Inspect the source. Keep the evidence behind your argument. A browser workspace and Python CLI for transparent, general-purpose legal research.

Open research workspace →Download source + MIT license

Find useful passages across different wording

Run several queries over your own source library, combine results using reciprocal rank fusion, or find literal phrases together in a bounded passage. Publication and source-quality flags stay visible alongside each match. Public research links let you continue on CourtListener or Google Scholar.

Separate evidence from interpretation

Record support, contrary evidence and factual distinctions. Label a passage as a holding, judicial reasoning, party argument or commentary. Exact quotes are checked against imported source text and saved with a source snapshot and SHA-256 hash. Export sources and notebooks as JSON or Markdown.

Your research stays with you

The browser workspace starts empty and keeps imported material in tab memory. Nothing is uploaded or automatically saved. Export your work before closing. Following a prepared research link sends its query to that provider. The optional sample texts are explicitly fictional.

A deeper local toolkit

The standard-library Python CLI adds SQLite FTS5, citation tracing, source-version comparison, research leads, evidence matrices and authority audits. Automated CourtListener discovery requires your own API access. No personal research or case corpus is bundled with the public release.

Review remains essential

This tool measures wording relevance and organizes researcher assessments. It does not authenticate sources, verify later treatment, certify controlling authority, or guarantee exhaustive research. Read the complete opinion and confirm publication, disposition and applicability.
