Symmetry
Forge.

An experimental Python toolkit for generating small combinatorial structures, computing their symmetries, and comparing simple measures of how unusual they are.

Open the symmetry labSource code ↗

The Heawood graph has 336 symmetries, and every node can be moved to every other.

What it does

  • Builds graphs, Steiner systems and relational structures, and computes their automorphism groups and canonical labels.
  • Uses its own refinement search by default, with optional nauty and SAT back ends for larger cases.
  • Runs logged experiments, from the Fano plane to a bounded attempt at constructions inspired by codes and the Monster group.

What it is not

The toolkit is for reproducible exploration and teaching. Its samples are starting points, not validated discoveries, and computing something is not the same as proving it.
