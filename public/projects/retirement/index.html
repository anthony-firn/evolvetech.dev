Project 01 · Investing · Open source

Retirement
Research.

Help people prepare for retirement and understand how savings could support spending, with the assumptions and trade-offs in view.

Open the income explorerRead the code and results ↗

The income explorer compares 15 strategies, including 60/40 and all-stock baselines.

Research, not advice.

The explorer shows simulations on partly reconstructed market history. Some tested strategies hold leveraged funds. The tax model is a simplified US/California retiree. Use it to understand trade-offs and to ask better questions, then check any plan with a fee-only fiduciary and a tax preparer.

The questions

  • How do income, costs, taxes, inflation and spending flexibility interact?
  • What changes in bad markets or when the future differs from the past?
  • When does added complexity provide enough benefit to justify its cost?
  • How can other people reproduce the work and challenge the assumptions?

The approach

Strategies are judged by the money a retiree can actually spend each month after tax, in today's dollars, and by how often the plan breaks: ending below its starting value, dipping below 70% of it, or running dry. Each strategy is re-run on 2,000 fresh 40-year histories and in stress cases where trend-following or gold earns less, only recent history repeats, or taxes are higher.

Failed ideas stay in the record. Reconstructed fund histories are documented, and observed results are kept separate from simulations. The repository includes the simulator, the searches, the data pipeline and a local planning app.

What to keep in mind

Backtests and simulations do not guarantee future income. Some fund histories are reconstructed from proxies, and leveraged funds behave differently in markets that have not happened before. Results from one model do not establish suitability for any particular person.
