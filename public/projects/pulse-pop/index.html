Pulse Pop.

Tap the shrinking pulses before they vanish. Chain hits into combos, earn coins and unlock new color skins in a 30-second round.

Play in the browserSource code ↗

A round in progress. It plays with a mouse or a touch screen.

How it is made

One Expo and React Native codebase targets iPhone, Android and the web. The game rules (scoring, combo caps, coin payouts and safe cosmetic unlocks) are covered by tests.

It has not been submitted to the app stores and has no ads, purchases, analytics or network services. Revive and double-coin buttons are free prototype actions.
