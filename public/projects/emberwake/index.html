Project 02 · Games · Playable prototype

Emberwake.

The light fades; the labyrinth does not. Explore seeded rooms, fight guardians, gather soulfire, choose blessings and rest at shrines as you push deeper into an endless crypt.

Play in the browserSource code ↗

The first room of a run.

How to play

  • WASD or arrow keys to move, the mouse to aim, and a left click or J to strike.
  • Space to dash, E to use a nearby shrine, Esc to pause, and 1, 2 or 3 to choose a blessing.
  • The browser version needs a keyboard and mouse and a computer with WebGL 2. It downloads about 40 MB the first time.

How it is made

Room geometry, lighting, combat particles and sound effects are generated while you play. Characters and props are low-poly models built from a reproducible Blender script, so every asset can be rebuilt from source. The game runs on Godot 4 and is exported to WebAssembly for this page.

It is an early prototype, not a finished or store-published game, and it has no accounts, ads or network services.
