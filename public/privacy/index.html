Privacy

EvolveTech is Anthony Firn’s independent software project in California. This notice explains the public website and the apps hosted here. Contact anthony@evolvetech.dev about privacy or your information. Do not email passwords, wallet keys, medical records or identity documents.

Visiting this site

Netlify and Bunny provide public hosting. They receive connection information such as IP addresses, requested URLs and device/browser information when serving requests. DNS and operational monitoring use additional infrastructure, including AWS. We do not add advertising trackers or analytics to the portfolio. Hosting security and access logs can still exist. Provider retention and backups vary; we do not promise immediate deletion or a fixed retention period that has not been verified.

What each app does

  • Fairground: account and recovery material is kept on your device. Posts, profiles and signed public events go to the relays you use; messages and media contact their respective services. Recipients can retain what you send. Wallet queries and transactions contact public blockchain services and swap providers. Blockchain addresses, amounts and transaction notes are public and cannot be erased by deleting your account. In-app card and bank checkout is not currently available on this deployment.
  • Evolve Health: records are encrypted locally. Optional sync nodes hold encrypted copies and see connection metadata. Shared recipients can read the information you authorize and may keep copies. Removing a node or local vault does not delete every remote copy. Unencrypted exports and recovery material need private storage. The website operator does not run a central health-record store for this deployment.
  • Evolve Inbox: the demonstration uses fictional messages. Email sent to this domain is different: the live private inbox stores message contents, sender/recipient information and attachments using Netlify Blobs and forwarding services. Authentication and security events can be logged. Authorized inbox access is private, not a public message board. Mail may be kept for correspondence, security and legal needs; request deletion of information you sent us by email.
  • Open Case Law: saved cases and notes stay in browser storage. Search queries are sent through EvolveTech to CourtListener. Queries can appear in hosting logs. Do not put confidential client or case facts into a public search.
  • Evolve LLM: inference runs locally and model downloads contact their hosts. If you enable web access, search terms and requested pages go to the named search/page providers. A search can disclose words from your question. Optional service keys are stored in your browser and sent to their provider.
  • Evolve Jobs: search rules and applicant information are kept locally until you choose external services or enable application submission. Job discovery contacts employer/provider endpoints; a submitted application discloses its contents to the employer and its systems.
  • Probate, retirement, games and engineering tools: answers, settings or drafts may be stored in your browser. Downloads save files to your device. Engineering peer sharing, mirror checks and external links contact the services or peers you select; network discovery can expose connection information.

Your choices and copies

Apps use browser storage for accounts, settings, backups and drafts. Use their export, deletion, sign-out or reset controls where available, and back up anything you need before clearing site data. Browser deletion cannot retract public posts, payments, delivered email, recipient exports or independent node/relay copies. The independent operator’s policies apply to its services. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information under our control or to raise a privacy complaint. We may need proportionate verification; do not send secrets. Applicable law may permit or require retention.

Advertising and legal requests

Fairground supports creator advertising and sponsorships; this notice does not claim that every relay, creator or external provider has identical practices. We do not sell information from the portfolio or private inbox. Information may be disclosed when you request it, to provide the described services, to protect against abuse, or when legally required. Encryption does not make a system immune to a breach or legal process.

Children and tracking signals

These experimental services are not directed to children under 13. Children under 13 should not create accounts, post or enter personal information here. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us. Adult-content controls are not verified age assurance. The portfolio does not use cross-site behavioral advertising and does not change its operation in response to Do Not Track signals. That statement does not describe every third-party service or settle every app’s legal obligations.
