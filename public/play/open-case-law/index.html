Three steps

  1. Find cases. Use keywords, a case name, or a citation. Narrow by court when useful.
  2. Read the original. Each result links to its CourtListener opinion. The preview is a discovery snippet, which can include dissenting or procedural text.
  3. Save what helps. Save cases with one click. Add a note or paste actual opinion text to preserve a quotation and page or paragraph reference.

What search covers

Live results are real records from CourtListener, provided by Free Law Project. The public feed returns a limited set of recent matches, up to 20, newest first. It is not a complete relevance-ranked search. Use “More on CourtListener” for relevance sorting and additional results. Zero matches does not establish that no authority exists. Some original opinion pages may require a browser security check.

Your work is saved on this device

Cases, imported text, and notes are stored in this browser’s IndexedDB. They are not uploaded. Search queries are sent to EvolveTech and CourtListener; hosting providers may log those requests. Following an original-opinion link visits that service. Clearing browser data, private browsing, switching devices, or changing website origin can remove or separate your saved work. Download a backup for portability.

Check the authority

Read the complete opinion and check publication, disposition, later treatment, and applicability. The tool does not verify whether a case is binding or still good law. Exact quotation checks compare your quote to text you supplied, not an authenticated court copy. A saved preview is not evidence of a holding.
