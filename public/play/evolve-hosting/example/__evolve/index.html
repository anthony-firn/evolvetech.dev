    02 / More than one home

    Choose where it runs.

    Checks contact the listed operators and compare their reported release metadata. They do not attest to remote HTML. The two published example copies are operated by EvolveTech; community participation is open.

    Know what travels

    Public app. Explicit dependencies.

    Mirrors distribute public files. A private API, mailbox or database needs its own operator and recovery plan. The support flow reads a public Algorand indexer and opens your own wallet.

    03 / Bring your own machine

    Your copy.
    Your choice of host.

    Node 22.18+ verifies the signed files before serving them. Activation preserves a previous release for rollback.

    First clone the repository, run npm ci and npm run build. Put the downloaded release and an independently trusted public key in that directory. Local serving binds to 127.0.0.1:4198. Public operators configure their own HTTPS proxy.

    04 / Keep it going
    Support is optional.
    Recipients are visible.
    Choose who to support and the amount for each. Fairground’s payment rail opens your wallet; funds go straight to recipients.
    Native USDC on Algorand, using your external wallet. Network fees apply. No automatic charge, custody, card checkout or promised hosting reward. Each recipient must be able to receive USDC before links are prepared.
