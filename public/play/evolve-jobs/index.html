Let your own device do the work.

The browser runs a Rust/WASM matching engine. The companion browser extension can apply to matching roles on standard Lever forms after you enable specific employers. An optional Node worker is also available. Your résumé and answers stay local until submitted directly to the employer. Unanswered questions, consent controls and CAPTCHA stop the attempt for review.

  1. Download the companion extension and load it unpacked in Chromium.
  2. Set your own pay, location and qualification rules.
  3. Export an application plan, import it into the extension and enable employers.
