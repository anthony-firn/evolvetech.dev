Report a privacy, security or content problem

Email anthony@evolvetech.dev. You do not need a Fairground account. This is a human-operated reporting channel, not an emergency service. Reporting does not require a donation or payment.

Tell us what needs attention

Include the app or page URL, an event ID or transaction ID when relevant, a description of the issue and a safe way to reply. Use a clear subject such as “Privacy request”, “Security report”, “Copyright report”, or “Intimate image removal”. Share the minimum information needed. Do not attach exploitative images of children, private keys, identity documents or medical records.

Intimate images shared without consent

You or your authorized representative can request removal without creating an account. Identify the image or video by URL or event ID, provide contact information, and state in good faith that you are the person depicted (or authorized to act for them) and that it was shared without consent. Avoid attaching the image itself. Applicable US law requires covered platforms to remove content and known identical copies within 48 hours of a valid request. This channel accepts requests; publishing it does not establish that every operational compliance requirement has been completed.

We can act on surfaces under our control, including this app and gateway. Independent media hosts and relays may hold separate copies, and a local hide/block action is not universal deletion. Include known copies so we can identify the relevant operators and scope.

Copyright

Identify the copyrighted work, the allegedly infringing material and its location, your contact information, your authority to act, and the basis for your complaint. Do not send misleading or knowingly false notices. This is a general copyright contact, not a representation that a designated DMCA agent has been registered or that a statutory safe harbor has been established. Appropriate notice, counter-notice and legal-process handling will depend on the activity and law.

Security

Describe the affected URL, impact and safe reproduction steps. Do not access other people’s records, publish secrets, disrupt service, move funds, or send a live exploit to another user. No bug bounty or broad authorization to test other people’s systems is offered. Security claims and privacy requests are handled according to the actual facts and applicable law.
