Keep control of your information

Save account recovery material privately before funding a wallet or relying on browser storage. We cannot reset self-custody keys or recover every lost file. Never give anyone your wallet signing key or recovery code. Sharing a document or publishing an event may give other people copies you cannot retract.

Voluntary project support

Support for the development fund goes to Anthony Firn for development and operating costs. It is voluntary project support, not an investment or a promise of returns, ownership, employment, services or future access. EvolveTech is not representing it as a tax-deductible charitable contribution. If you choose a relay split, part goes directly to independent relay operators; their status and responsibilities may differ.

Review the recipient, split, amount, asset, network and fees before signing. Payments use native USDC on Algorand; a small ALGO reserve pays network costs. USDC can depart from its target value or be restricted by issuer controls. Blockchain payments are public even when you hide a social receipt. Confirmed transactions cannot simply be reversed by this website.

Contact anthony@evolvetech.dev with the transaction ID if you sent development support in error. Do not include private keys. A refund requires a separate transfer and verification; we cannot promise recovery of wrong-network payments or funds sent to another recipient. Nothing here removes rights you have under applicable law.
