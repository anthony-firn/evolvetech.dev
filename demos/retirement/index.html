Retirement Income Explorer.

How much could savings pay each month, and how often would a plan break? Compare the withdrawal strategies the research tested, on 2,000 reshuffled 40-year market histories and seven stress cases.

Research, not advice. These are simulations on partly reconstructed market history (1972–2026). Several strategies hold leveraged funds (TQQQ, TYD, UGL, TMF) that can lose most of their value quickly. Taxes assume a low-bracket California retiree with a taxable account unless you pick the middle-bracket case. Past and simulated results do not predict future income. Check any plan with a fee-only fiduciary and a tax preparer.

How each strategy pays

Base pay: a fixed share of the starting value each year, paid monthly and raised with inflation. Bonus pay: at each year-end, gains above a threshold move into a T-bill bucket that pays out over several years. Guardrail: if the account falls below a set share of its inflation-adjusted start, base pay is cut until it recovers. The strategies differ in their funds and in those settings; the reference rows use the same rules at a 4% base.
